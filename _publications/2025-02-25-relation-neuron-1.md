This work identifies and characterizes relation-specific neurons in large language models — neurons that store explicit knowledge about semantic relations, with a particular focus on multilingual contexts. We develop a pipeline for localizing such neurons in Llama-2 (7B & 13B) using a tailored dataset, and validate the findings through controlled generation experiments.
